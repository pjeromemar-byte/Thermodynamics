Sensible heat changes temperature: Q = m·c·ΔT. Latent heat changes phase at constant temperature: Q = m·L.

QuantityValue
c ice2.09 kJ/kg·K
c water4.18 kJ/kg·K

L vaporization2260 kJ/kg

Kinetic theory: temperature measures the average kinetic energy of particles. During a phase change the added energy raises potential energy (bonds break), so the temperature holds still.

First Law: Q = ΔU + W. For an ideal gas ΔU = n·Cv·ΔT with Cv = (f/2)R.

Ideal gas law: PV = nRT. Kinetic theory: average KE = (3/2)kT and vrms = √(3RT/M).
